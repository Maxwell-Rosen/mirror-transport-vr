# Controls

In the entry foyer, place a controls graphic beside the doorway, opposite the welcome panel. Show the controls for the device in use and keep the graphic readable from a chair. [Foyer layout](SETTING.md)

## Move around the museum

| Action | Meta Quest 3 | Desktop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Look around | Turn your head. | Move the mouse to look and aim. |
| Walk along the floor | Take physical steps within your play space. | W or ↑ moves forward, S or ↓ moves back, A or ← steps left, and D or → steps right. |
| Sprint | — | Hold **Shift** while moving. Release it to walk again. |
| Teleport | Tilt the right thumbstick in any direction and point the right controller. Release the stick to land at the floor circle. | — |

While you hold the stick off-centre, an arc from the right controller follows your aim. A circle marks clear floor within a four-metre horizontal radius of your feet. To cancel, aim at a wall so the circle disappears, then release. The headset also tracks leaning and physical steps. You can lean toward a label, walk or teleport into a sculpture, and step back for the full view. Gallery walls and closed entrances stop movement. You can reach each exhibit from a chair using the right controller.

The broader exhibit plan also includes optional flight for viewing the taller displays, with ascent, descent, a level horizon and a control to return to the floor beneath you. [Exhibit plan](PLAN.md)

## Use an exhibit

Point the right controller at a button and pull the index trigger, or aim with the mouse and left-click. To move a slider or probe, hold the trigger or left mouse button, move along the slider or rail, then release. [Gallery 1 launch controls and plots](gallery-1/README.md#launch-controls-and-plots)

On the Gallery 2 bead stand, select **Pause** to inspect the orbits or **Next phase** to jump to the hold and release. **Play** resumes from the selected time. **Replay** returns to the opening frame. Use the mouse or right trigger. On desktop, stand near the front of the rings and use **Tab** or **Shift+Tab** to focus either button and **Enter** to activate it. [Between Bounces](gallery-2/README.md)

At the Gallery 3 lectern, choose **Send a pulse** to launch a wave around the fixed rings. Switch dilation on or off to compare the same instant. **See steady state** previews the final glow and **Return to wave** resumes the wave. **View midplane** changes the slice and **Next ring** moves the probe. Use the mouse or right trigger. Select a colour on the mask plinth or its companion print to isolate a shell. On the time-step relief, choose one of five magnetic moments μ. Switch **Time dilation** off and on to see the valleys rise into a common floor. **Throat slope**, **Midplane** and **Slice minimum** move the probe and update its two clocks. At the next station choose a time limit in nanoseconds to compare both contour volumes at once. **Time floor**, **10% ion core** and **Use lamp cell** add context to that comparison. At each station, **Tab** and **Shift+Tab** select a visible control and **Enter** activates it. [Time Dilation](gallery-3/README.md)

At the Gallery 4 energy rail, **Cut cell** selects the sole trapped corner in a passing cell. Choose **Wall corner** to trace an open path. **Beam well** selects the slow ion near the midplane. Click or use the right trigger on the mask to set the neighbouring energy line, change slices to inspect the same velocity cell, or use **Tip zoom** for finer pointing. On desktop, Tab to the plot and use the arrows to step by one cell. At any of the three stations, Tab and Shift+Tab cycle through controls and Enter activates one.

At the velocity-grid board, **Loss-cone tip** enlarges the same cells on the computational and physical axes. Toggle the grid lines and mask boundary over the ion distribution independently. At the axial grid, switch between **Uniform spacing** and **Simulation grid** with a probe fixed at **Midplane** or **Steep flank**. [Gallery 4 trials](gallery-4/README.md)

In Gallery 5, switch **Beam / Maxwellian** at the plinth, choose **Follow band**, and use **Next slice** to inspect the throat or sheath. At the density and potential desks, step or play saved frames and zoom the final full-dynamics phase. At the end wall, a numbered button selects both runs at that mirror ratio. Use the mouse or right trigger, or Tab / Shift+Tab and Enter at the nearest desk. Leaving the room holds the current samples. [Gallery 5 visitor trials](gallery-5/README.md)

## Title screen and pause menu

On the title screen, choose **Enter the museum** to begin or **Exit** to close the game. In either menu, use the mouse or Tab and Enter on desktop. On Quest, aim the right controller and pull its index trigger. A yellow ring marks where the ray meets the menu.

When you enter, the title and its music fade to black, then the foyer appears as the Promenade fades in. Press **Esc** on desktop or **A** on the right Quest controller to pause the visit and read all controls. The music continues quietly while paused and returns to its usual level as you resume. Drag **Volume** or select **Mute audio** to adjust all museum sound. On a keyboard, Tab to the slider and use the arrow keys. Unmuting restores the selected volume. **Resume visit** or the same pause button continues from the same spot. Choose **Reset to title**, then **Enter the museum** to start a fresh visit on the foyer mat.
