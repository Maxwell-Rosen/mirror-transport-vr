# Controls

Keep the controls simple. Have a graphic on a wall to tell players about the controls

The players will be sitting down in a chair in VR or standing up. The player is walking on the floor.

WASD to move on computer, or left joystick. It would be good to have the "jump to position" mode in VR, as this reduces motion sickness, but allowing physical walking in the space to move the player and camera too
VR, triggers are interact, or left click on mouse.

Holding select resets to the beginning of the museum, where a circular progress wheel pops up to show this.
