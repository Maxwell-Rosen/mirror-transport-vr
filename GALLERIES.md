## Gallery 4

**Which ions can reach a wall?** Pick an ion on the saved Gkeyll mask and trace its path. Find the trapped corner in a passing cell and the slow ion confined by the beam-driven electric well. Compare the velocity grid on two rulers, then move 400 axial cells into place near the steep throat flanks. [Playable trial and simulation checks](gallery-4/README.md)

## Gallery 5

Simple mirror results with beams vs Maxwellian. PRE results, with distribution functions. Scanning mirror ratio study. Ambipolar potential analysis. Explain that the ion confinement time predicts the fusion power, as the lawson criterion tells us, we must know the confinement time.

Show the source on top of the distribution function and how it's orbit distributed

## Gallery 6

Tandem mirror simulations. Show the distribution function. Show the sources. Show the geometry thorugh a sculpture with the mirror coils, similar to the one used in gallery 1.

Explain and show how a tandem mirror enhances the confinement time for the ions
