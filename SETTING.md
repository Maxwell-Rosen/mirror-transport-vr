# Setting

The player is inside a museum. This is a simple museum with low poly graphics. There are columns. The walls are a neutral grey-beige. The ceiling is tall. The museum features octagonal columns, with square bases followed by circular modling. The walls have a beak molding at the floor and extend quite tall. There are benches in the museum to give the appearance that one may sit down, as if this place may exist in the real world. The entire game takes place inside this museum.

Players start in a entry foyer, where there is a graphic on the wall of the controls. The player starts facing a doorway without a door, which they may walk through to enter the main atrium. The atrium is a long hallway and exhibits lie to the left and right in seperate rooms. The doorways to those rooms are quite large and inviting. The atrium has a pointed roof. The left and right sides of the triangular roof have skylights letting in light from above. Plants live in the atrium with a peaceful fountain (no sound). The atrium is an intermediate space while walking through the various exhibits. This space is peaceful, letting players take a step back before moving onto the next exhibit.

Exhibit number 1 is INTRO_MIRROR_INTERACTIVE.md, which is the first entrance on the left as players enter the atrium.

Each exhibit has distinctive text telling players what each exhibit is.
